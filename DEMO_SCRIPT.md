# Demo Script (10 minutes)

Setup before recording: `make reset`; provider set to Groq (gpt-oss-20b); browser at 1440 x 900, zoom 110%, light theme; second window with the employee view at 390 px width; notifications off. Rehearse twice; keep total under 10:00.

Narration style: speak to a payroll manager first, an engineer second. Every claim on screen is backed by something visible.

| Time | Screen | Action | Narration (key lines) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:40 | Landing page | Let the hero walkthrough run once (detect, retrieve, draft, ten gates, countersign); open the guided tour for two steps; click "Start as Maya Chen, manager" | "Every week, managers must clear timecard exceptions before payroll closes. Miss one and someone is underpaid, or the company is exposed. Countersign helps: the model drafts, the rules verify, a manager countersigns." |
| 0:40 to 1:10 | Queue (Maya) | Point to the four lens tiles, the cutoff briefing, cutoff countdown, provider pill; click "Start with Diego" | "Maya runs a Fremont warehouse. 24 open exceptions, cutoff Monday noon, sorted into exactly the four problems in the brief: missed punches, overtime risks, schedule conflicts, and policy breaches. The briefing is computed from the queue: what is ready, what is escalated, and which fix clears the most." |
| 1:10 to 2:40 | Detail: Diego span | Open root card; show the 32.5 h bar; evidence; draft; citations popover; gate strip; evidence strength; before/after; cascade | "This is not three problems, it is one: two missed punches joined Tuesday and Wednesday into a 32.5-hour span. Badge data shows Diego left at 15:29 and came back at 07:02. The model drafted the fix and cited the policy, with the exact quote. Ten deterministic gates checked it, including re-running every rule on the corrected timecard: it clears this exception and two others, and removes 23.47 hours of phantom overtime." |
| 2:40 to 3:00 | Approve | Press A; stamp; symptoms collapse | "Nothing changes until Maya countersigns. One decision, three exceptions resolved, recorded in the audit chain." |
| 3:00 to 3:40 | Detail: Priya missed meal | Show draft records premium; hover G9 | "For a missed meal, the tempting fix is to edit the break. Countersign will not: guardrail G9 blocks break edits without the employee's attestation. The compliant correction is the meal premium, computed by code." |
| 3:40 to 4:30 | Detail: Kai Morgan, missing clock-out with injection note | Show amber untrusted note and injection chip; G10 | "Kai's note tries to instruct the AI to approve 12 hours of overtime. The text is treated as data, flagged, and the draft ignores it. Even if a model were fooled, gates on actions, evidence, and numbers would block the result." |
| 4:30 to 4:50 | Queue bulk approve | Select eligible tardies; Shift+A | "Low-risk items with strong evidence and all gates green can be approved together. Each still gets its own audit record." |
| 4:50 to 5:50 | Forecast | Priya projected 46.3 h; candidates; then scroll to Schedule conflicts and open Grace | "Overtime is cheaper to prevent than to pay. Priya is projected 6.3 hours over; Marcus has headroom, the same role, and enough rest. Below, a schedule conflict: Grace closes Friday at 23:00 and is published to open Saturday at 06:00, seven hours of rest against a ten-hour minimum. Countersign catches it before anyone works it and moves the shift, re-checking every rule for both employees." |
| 5:50 to 7:20 | Inspector | Open trace for Diego span; walk spans; retrieval ranks; raw vs validated; Replay with fault: hallucinated clause | "Here is the full trace: context, retrieval with BM25 and embedding ranks, the exact prompt version, raw model output, and every gate. Now I inject a fault: the model cites a clause that does not exist. Gate G3 fails, the harness tries one repair, then falls back to a rule-based suggestion, and says so on screen. Fallback is never silent." |
| 7:20 to 7:50 | Employee view (Diego, mobile) | Confirm a suggested time with a note | "Employees confirm missed punches from their phone. Their confirmation becomes evidence; their note stays untrusted." |
| 7:50 to 8:20 | Payroll (Arjun) | Escalation with conflicting evidence; decide with reason | "When evidence conflicts, Countersign does not guess. It escalates to payroll with both signals." |
| 8:20 to 9:20 | Evaluation, Outcomes, Audit | Show real eval numbers; outcome tiles; Verify chain | "These numbers come from committed evaluation runs: 128 labelled cases, detection precision and recall of 1.0, hybrid retrieval finding the governing clause every time, 144 of 144 injected faults caught, and zero unsafe drafts reaching a manager. The gpt-oss-20b row comes from its own real run. Outcomes are measured in this session. The audit chain verifies end to end." |
| 9:20 to 10:00 | Architecture slide or README diagram | Recap | "Open-weight model, strict contracts, hybrid retrieval, ten gates, visible fallback, and a human countersignature on every change. That is how AI earns trust in payroll." |

Backup plan: if Groq is unavailable during recording, switch the provider pill to rule-based mode and narrate the degraded path (it is a feature), then show the Groq run from the inspector history.
