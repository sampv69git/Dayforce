Introducing Countersign, where artificial intelligence does the drafting, deterministic rules ensure accuracy, and human judgement makes the final call.
Every week, before payroll closes, managers must clear timecard exceptions by hand.
Missed punches, surprise overtime, schedule conflicts, and policy breaches.
Get one wrong, and someone is underpaid, or the company is exposed.
Countersign is a timecard exception agent built for exactly that moment.
Rules detect the problem, and an open-weight model drafts the fix.
Ten deterministic gates verify it, and nothing changes until a manager countersigns.
We built it around the challenge brief, line by line.
Missed punches, overtime risks, schedule conflicts, policy breaches.
Each one is detected by deterministic rules and answered with a compliant correction.
The harness is visible from the first screen. Hover any gate to see exactly what it checks.
And first-time visitors get a guided tour, so nobody gets lost.
A new manager registers with a name, an email, and the site they run.
Password rules are checked live, and passwords are stored with scrypt hashing.
